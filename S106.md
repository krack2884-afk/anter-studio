# S106: Be the Eye of the Storm

**Published:** 20260801

Tyler — Don't confuse a loud world with a divine assignment. Your strength is not measured by how many people you unsettle; it's measured by whether you can stay whole while everything around you gets noisy.

Your marriage is one of the places that strength has to become real. Choose peace over being right. Let your wife see a man who can hold firm without turning hard, and who can love without abandoning himself.

Be the eye of the storm—not another storm.
