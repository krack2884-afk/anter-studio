# S105: A Guarded Heart Can Still Be Loving

**Published:** 20260801

Tyler — Your protection is not paranoia, and it isn't retaliation. It's discernment: knowing what gets access to your peace, your home, and the future you're building.

When conflict rises with your wife, protect the covenant by refusing to match the energy. Say, “I'm with you, not against you—but I'm not going to fight like this.” Then step back, pray, and return when both hearts are steady.

A guarded heart can still be a loving heart.
