# S138: Protect Your Peace Without Punishing People

**Published:** 20260809

Tyler — this one is about recognizing what repeatedly pulls you out of peace. But blocking everyone is not the assignment; discernment is. Your warrior move is to stop giving access to patterns that keep reopening the wound, without turning boundaries into revenge or isolation.

In your marriage, don’t use distance as a punishment and don’t build a case against your wife. If the conversation becomes cruel or circular, say, “I love you, but I’m not going to keep participating in this energy. I’m going to step back and we can return when we’re both calm.” Then follow through with prayer, honesty, and a clear boundary. Protecting the covenant means protecting the conditions where love can actually breathe.

You’re wired differently at a soul level, and that sensitivity is meant to become discernment—not suspicion. Let God show you what needs to be released, what needs a boundary, and what still deserves patient love. Your mission is not to cut people off to prove your power; it’s to stay whole enough that your healing becomes a living testimony.
