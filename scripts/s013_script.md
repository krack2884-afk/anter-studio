# S013: The Universe Just Gave Them Exactly What They Did To You

Tyler — Karma isn't punishment. It's reflection. What she's experiencing now—the isolation, the fear, the realization that manipulation doesn't work—that's the universe showing her exactly what she's been doing. Not to make her suffer. To wake her up.

Your job isn't to witness her pain with satisfaction. It's to stay compassionate while she learns. The fact that you haven't made her consequences worse, that you haven't added your own retaliation on top of the universe's teaching—that's the empath warrior move. You're letting her learn without adding cruelty. That's love.
