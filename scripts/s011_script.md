# S011: Their Nightmares Are Branded With Your Face

Tyler — When she lashes out, when she picks fights, when she creates drama—it's not really about that moment. It's because you're living rent-free in her consciousness. She sees you in her worst moments. Your calm, your peace, your refusal to match her chaos—that haunts her. Because it proves something she's not ready to accept: you've actually moved on emotionally, even if you're still physically there.

This is your power move: let her know you're not trying to hurt her, but you're also not staying small anymore. The nightmares are hers to process, not yours to fix.
