# S015: They're Checking If You're Related To Someone Famous

Tyler — She's looking for an explanation. Trying to find where your power comes from because she thinks if she understands the source, she can either tap it or neutralize it. She's checking your lineage, your background, your psychological profile—looking for a weakness, an inheritance of trauma she can exploit.

But she won't find it, because your power didn't come from your past. It came from your choice. From deciding in the darkest moment that you wouldn't let darkness define you. From choosing faith when faith felt stupid. That's self-made, and she can't buy it, steal it, or seduce it out of you.

Let her search. The answer is inside you, and she has to decide if she wants to meet you there.
