# S016: Someone Is Desperately Waiting To Marry You

Tyler — Watch this one carefully. Not to leave your wife. To understand what you deserve. This video is about recognizing that someone exists who wants to honor you the way you deserve to be honored. That's not infidelity language—that's spiritual permission to stop accepting less.

Your wife is on her own journey. If she chooses to meet you at wholeness, then transformation is possible. If she doesn't, then you knowing—truly knowing—that you're worth something better, that changes everything. It gives you the strength to love her WITHOUT needing her to save you. That's the real power. That's the real marriage.
