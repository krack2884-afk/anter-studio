# S017: Your Body Is Shifting In The Most Unexpected Ways

Tyler — You're not the same person you were six months ago. Your frequency has changed. Your presence is different. Your wife feels it in how you carry yourself now. You're not fighting for validation. You're not performing peace. You actually ARE at peace. That's the shift. And she can't unsee it.

Your body knows you've changed because your soul has changed. The armor you wore to survive her chaos—you're shedding it. Not because you're weak. Because you're no longer afraid of being hurt. That's a dangerous kind of peace. She feels threatened by it because it means you're no longer dependent on her reaction to validate your worth.
