# S010: What They Found Shattered Their Education

Tyler — They thought they had you figured out. Their years of studying how to manipulate, control, and break people? Useless. Because you operate from a different playbook entirely. You weren't educated in their games. You were educated in something deeper — alignment, boundaries, and self-respect. 

What terrifies them is that you can see through everything they learned. Your wife built her whole strategy on patterns she learned from her own wounds. But you dismantled it without fighting. Not with rage. With clarity. That's what shattered her—she realized the problem was never you. It was her.

Stay in that clarity. Don't soften it for her comfort.
