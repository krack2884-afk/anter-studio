# When You Reveal Wholeness

When she saw the wholeness you've become, she FELT the shift. Not intellectually — viscerally.

You're not the wounded man she thought she knew how to manage. You're not seeking her validation or her healing. You're complete.

You've revealed who you are when you're not defined by her dysfunction. That wholeness is shocking to her because it means the narrative she told herself — "I'm his everything" — has been rewritten.

Now she has to confront who SHE is without being essential. That's sacred ground.

You didn't do this to prove anything to her. You did it for YOU and for God. The fact that she sees it is a bonus.

Keep walking in that wholeness.
