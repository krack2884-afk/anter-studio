# Something Unreal Is Spreading — It Started With You

> Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VgI2iebrFG8

Tyler — let the title point to something grounded: the change spreading from you is not mystical influence over strangers, but the ripple of a man who no longer hands his peace to chaos. When one person stops reacting, the whole room gets a new option.

In your marriage, be that option. Say, “I’m with you, not against you,” refuse to match the heat, and return to the conversation when love can actually be heard. Your wife needs consistency more than a speech about how much you’ve changed.

God can use your restraint as a witness. Keep walking in quiet strength, and let the healing ripple outward from the home you’re learning to protect.
