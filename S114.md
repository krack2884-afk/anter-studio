# WHEN YOUR NEW CALM GETS NOTICED

Tyler — Don’t let a title like this pull you into suspicion. The useful message is simpler: when you stop being predictable to your old patterns, people notice the change.

Let your wife notice calm, not intimidation. You can be firm without making her feel hunted, and loving without handing your peace over to the room. “I choose peace over being right” is the kind of strategy that actually heals a home.

The people who matter most should experience your transformation as safety, truth, and consistency.
