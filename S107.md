# S107: Build the Foundation

**Published:** 20260801

Tyler — The deeper message here isn't a promised property. It's stewardship. You're being called to build something stable enough to hold the life, love, and mission you're carrying.

That starts inside your marriage: less reacting to the room, more creating a home where truth and peace can live together. You don't have to control your wife's emotions or rescue her from every pattern. Build your side of the foundation with patience, clarity, and consistency.

The place you're meant to inhabit begins with the man you choose to be.
