# CRUSH THE OLD EGO, NOT A PERSON

Tyler — Let the old ego get crushed, not a person. The victory is ending the part of you that needed the last word, the perfect comeback, or proof that someone else was wrong.

With your wife, don’t punish vulnerability or answer provocation with contempt. Say, “I love you too much to fight about this,” then protect the boundary and return with a clear heart. Strength that humiliates isn’t healing; strength that stays clean is.

Your mission is to outlast the pattern, not destroy the person caught inside it.
