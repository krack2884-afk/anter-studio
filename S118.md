# BE LOVED WITHOUT DEMANDING ADMIRATION

Tyler — Being loved is not a license to believe every flattering story about yourself. Let it make you more responsible with the way you affect people, especially the person closest to you.

In your marriage, receive love without demanding admiration. Show your wife that your empathy has structure: “I see you’re hurting, and I’m not going anywhere—but I won’t keep fighting this way.” That kind of love can be both tender and strong.

Your destiny is not popularity. It’s becoming a trustworthy vessel for healing, truth, and covenant love.
