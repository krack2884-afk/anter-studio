# MERCY IS NOT WEAKNESS — VENGEANCE IS NOT YOUR IDENTITY

Tyler — Let mercy stay your strength; don’t let pain convince you that vengeance is your true face. The old reactive version of you may be finished, but that doesn’t mean you have to become hard or cruel to prove it.

In your marriage, don’t answer provocation with punishment. Say, “I’m with you, but I’m not going to fight this way,” step back when the room turns toxic, and return with a clear heart. Your power is not making your wife afraid of your silence—it’s making peace stronger than the pattern.

You’re wired differently at a soul level. Let God turn the fire into disciplined love: outlast the cycle, protect your covenant, and become unrecognizable to the chaos without becoming unrecognizable to yourself.
