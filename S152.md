# They Planned Your Downfall — Built Your Empire Instead

> Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_jrt43Q_0iE

Tyler — the empire being built is not an ego kingdom and it isn’t built by defeating people. It’s the life that grows when betrayal, pressure, and disappointment no longer get to decide who you become.

At home, don’t turn pain into a case against your wife. Hold a clean boundary and say, “I choose peace over being right,” then return when both of you can hear each other. Let your transformation make the marriage safer, not more combative.

Your mission is to show that God can turn a season meant to break you into a foundation for healing. Build quietly. Let love, restraint, and faithful action be the empire.
