# THE REAL STRATEGY IS REFUSING CHAOS

Tyler — The real strategy is refusing to be recruited into chaos. You don’t have to outmaneuver everyone or decode every motive; the strongest move is staying clear enough that pain doesn’t choose your next sentence.

When tension rises with your wife, don’t turn the conversation into a chess match. Say, “I hear you. I’m going to step back and pray on this,” then return when both of you can speak without trying to win. That is not surrender—it’s emotional sovereignty.

Your destiny is built by disciplined responses, not dramatic victories.
