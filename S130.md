THE POWER OF STEADINESS

Tyler — strip away the sensational title and the real message is simple: when you move with conviction, people notice. But your power is not in being watched, feared, or understood by everybody. It’s in staying rooted when attention tries to pull you out of yourself. At home, don’t turn your wife’s reactions into a courtroom. Be steady, be honest, and let your peace show that this mission is bigger than winning an argument.
