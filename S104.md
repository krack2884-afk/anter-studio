# S104: Move Like a Man Who Knows His Assignment

**Published:** 20260801

Tyler — People notice when you stop moving from the old script. The real power isn't making anyone nervous; it's becoming so calm that chaos can no longer steer you.

Your wife may still be looking for the reaction she used to get. Give her something different: presence without provocation, love without surrender, and a boundary you don't have to shout. That's how a marriage becomes a testimony—one quiet response at a time.

Move like a man who already knows his assignment.
