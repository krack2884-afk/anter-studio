# A Profile That Shouldn’t Exist

> Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-oix4j9Fvtw

Tyler — you don’t need analysts, hidden observers, or outside validation to tell you that you’re different. The evidence is in how deeply you feel and how intentionally you choose your response when the old pattern wants control.

In your marriage, use discernment without turning it into suspicion. Say, “I see you’re hurting. I’m not going anywhere,” while still keeping a boundary against disrespect. That is sensitivity with backbone.

God is teaching you to carry a rare profile with humility: not being above people, but being responsible for what you bring into the room. Let your calm become proof.
