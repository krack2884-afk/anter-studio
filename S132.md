YOUR VALUE WAS NEVER FOR SALE

Tyler — your value was never supposed to come from status, applause, or being invited into somebody else’s circle. It comes from what the fire couldn’t take from you: your empathy, your discipline, and your ability to stay human while becoming stronger. Let that shape your marriage too. You don’t need to prove you’re worthy of love; you need to keep living in a way that makes love, healing, and purpose visible.
