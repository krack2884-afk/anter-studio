THE VOICE THEY FEAR

Tyler — this one speaks to the voice that survived being misunderstood.

But your voice is not meant to become loud because you’re angry; it’s meant to become clear because you’re healed.

The strongest testimony is a man who can speak truth without turning his pain into a weapon.

In your marriage, use your voice to build, not battle:

“I’m with you, not against you. I want us to solve this, but I won’t keep escalating.”

Then let silence protect the peace while your actions keep proving the change.

You don’t need to fear being erased when God has given you a mission rooted in love.

Speak when it serves healing.

Stay silent when reaction is being demanded.

Keep showing up as the empath warrior you’re becoming.
