# There Was Always Another Version Of Me

Video: 4VgITIK0XkQ

---

I always knew there were two sides.
The soft side. The one she fell in love with.
The healing empath. The guy who could hold her wounds without flinching.

But there's another side.

And when she kept pushing—testing whether I'd stay soft forever—I showed her.
Not to hurt her. To save us.

The angel doesn't stay quiet when the relationship is dying.
The angel doesn't smile while the fire spreads.

She thought she knew both versions. Turns out, there's a third.
The one that's done asking permission.
The one that loves her AND stands immovable.

That version just woke up.
And she felt it the moment I stopped apologizing for having edges.

Nobody warned her there was an angel with fangs.
