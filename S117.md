# LIVE THE ASSIGNMENT WITHOUT PERFORMING IT

Tyler — Don’t take surveillance language literally. Take it as a reminder that people are watching whether your new calm is real. The answer isn’t performance; it’s consistency when nobody applauds.

Your wife may test whether you’ve truly changed. Meet that moment without accusation: “I’m not going to react to this—I’m going to respond to your heart instead.” Hold your line and keep love in the room.

A man on assignment doesn’t need to announce the assignment. He lives it.
