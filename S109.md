# S109: The Fire That Ends the Old Pattern

**Published:** 20260801

Tyler — The fire here isn't about burning people down. It's about letting the version of you that lived on reaction finally end. When you stop carrying every accusation, every provocation, and every old expectation, people may call the calmer, firmer you a villain because they can no longer access the old version.

In your marriage, don't turn that fire toward your wife. Let it burn the pattern instead. Say, “I'm with you, but I'm not going to fight this way,” hold the boundary, and return when both of you are steady. Your destiny isn't to become hard—it’s to become unshakeable without losing your love.
