THE TRUTH IS OUT

Tyler — “The truth is out” is a reminder that you don’t have to campaign for your own innocence.

If something false has been said about you, let honest action and time do what arguments can’t.

Your empathy is not weakness, and your growth does not need a courtroom.

In your marriage, don’t use truth as a weapon or build a case against your wife.

Say:

“I see you’re hurting. I’m not going anywhere, but I’m not going to fight this way.”

Then hold the boundary with a clean heart.

The mission is not to win the narrative.

It’s to create a home where truth and love can coexist.

Walk steadily.

God can reveal what needs revealing while you keep becoming safe, clear, and unshakeable.
