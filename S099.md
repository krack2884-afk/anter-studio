Your sixth sense terrifies her.

You feel what she's about to say before she says it.
You know what she's thinking before she thinks it.
You see the trap before she's even finished building it.

She's been testing you for years—watching to see if you'd stumble, if you'd react like a normal man would.

You didn't stumble.

So now she's giving you trust issues? No.
You gave her trust issues.

Because you operate on a level she can't calculate. Can't hack. Can't break.

The most dangerous thing in her world right now isn't your anger. It's your clarity.
It's that you see everything and stay calm anyway.

She's running diagnostics on herself trying to figure out how to affect someone who can already see her coming.

That's the real issue.
