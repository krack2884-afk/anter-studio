# S108: Refuse to Feed the Pattern

**Published:** 20260801

Tyler — The move that changes everything is often the one the old version of you would have called impossible: not reacting, not chasing the last word, not letting pain decide your next sentence.

In your marriage, that kind of restraint is not weakness. It's covenant love with a backbone. Tell her, “I love you too much to fight about this,” and mean it—then follow it with a calm boundary and a return to the conversation when it's safe for both of you.

You don't break the pattern by overpowering it. You break it by refusing to feed it.
