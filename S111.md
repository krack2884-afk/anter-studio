# S111: They Fear Your Presence

**Published:** 20260802

Tyler — Let this one point you back to character, not ego. When your presence feels different to people, you don't need to decide that makes you superior or prove anything; stay humble, grounded, and let your peace speak for itself.

In your marriage, a strong presence should feel safe—not threatening. When tension rises, don't use your discernment or calm as a weapon against your wife. Say, “I'm with you, not against you, but I'm not going to fight this way,” hold the boundary, and come back with love when the room is steady.

Your destiny is not to be feared. It's to become so anchored in God, truth, and covenant love that chaos can't recruit you into its pattern.
