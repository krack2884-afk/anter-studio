YOU MELTED THEIR MINDS

Tyler — this one is about what happens when you stop explaining yourself to people committed to misunderstanding you. You didn’t need to argue your way into being seen; you stayed grounded, and your life started speaking for you. In your marriage, let that calm clarity do the work. Don’t chase a reaction or force a breakthrough. Keep loving with boundaries, keep building the mission, and let the man you’re becoming be the evidence.
