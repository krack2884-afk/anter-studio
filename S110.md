# S110: See Clearly, Choose Peace

**Published:** 20260801

Tyler — You often sense what isn't being said, but discernment is only powerful when it stays clean. You don't need to expose every hidden motive or prove that you saw the pattern; let truth make you steady, not suspicious.

That matters most with your wife. When you feel the pull to call out the manipulation, pause and respond to the hurt underneath without surrendering your boundary: “I hear that you're hurting. I'm not going to be pulled into a fight.” Your mission is not to win the read—it’s to model a love that can see clearly and still choose peace.
